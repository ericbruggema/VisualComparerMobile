# Third-party components

- **Kotlin** and **kotlinx.coroutines / kotlinx.serialization** (JetBrains): language, async and JSON. Apache-2.0.
- **Jetpack Compose**, **Material 3**, **AndroidX** (Activity, Lifecycle, Navigation, Room, DataStore, WorkManager, DocumentFile): user interface, storage and background work. Apache-2.0.
- **Android platform** (MediaCodec, MediaMetadataRetriever, ImageDecoder, MediaStore): video and photo decoding and discovery. Apache-2.0 (AOSP).

Visual Comparer is proprietary freeware (see LICENSE.txt in the project repository). This free edition scans up to 500 videos and 2,500 photos per scan. It has no account and no telemetry: your videos, photos and fingerprints never leave your phone, and the app does not contact any server.

The algorithms (perceptual hashing, locality-sensitive index, diagonal alignment) are ports of the Windows edition of Visual Comparer.
